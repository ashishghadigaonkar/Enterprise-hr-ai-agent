# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

1. Overview
Full-time employees receive standard Paid Time Off (PTO) accruals based on tenure. Standard accrued PTO for full-time employees ranges between 15 to 25 days per calendar year.

2. Request Procedure
Employees must request PTO at least 5 business days in advance using the self-service HR portal. Requests longer than 5 consecutive business days require manager approval.

3. Rollover Policy
A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the next calendar year. Any additional unused PTO days will expire on December 31st.

4. Parental & Bereavement Leave
Parental leave provides up to 12 weeks of paid leave for new parents. Bereavement leave provides up to 5 days of paid leave.
