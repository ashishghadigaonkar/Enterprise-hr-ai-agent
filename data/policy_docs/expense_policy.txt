# Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim business-related expenses including client meals, travel, software subscriptions, and home office equipment, up to their designated expense limit.

2. Receipt Requirement
All expense claims exceeding $25.00 require itemized receipts attached to the submission.

3. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are processed within 10 business days and disbursed via direct deposit alongside bi-weekly payroll.

4. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, parking fines, and unapproved hardware purchases are strictly non-reimbursable.
